FREEZE OR PUSH?
ILLUSTRATIVE
WAVE MANAGEMENT
You control a minion wave in two ways
You freeze it or you push it. Your choice decides who wins the lane.
❄ FREEZE · 1/3
The wave waits outside your tower
Keep the wave just in front of your own tower, but never let it cross the red line: your tower's range.
❄ FREEZE · 2/3 · HOW?
Only last-hit
Don't auto-attack minions for free. Let the enemy wave keep about 4 extra casters: it leans toward you but never reaches your tower.
❄ FREEZE · 3/3 · WHY?
The enemy has to come to you
To farm, they must walk deep into your side. Far from their tower, your jungler catches them.
PUSH ➜ · 1/3
Clear the wave fast
Spend your abilities on minions. The enemy wave dies in a few seconds and your wave starts marching.
PUSH ➜ · 2/3
The wave crashes into their tower
Their tower kills your minions. The enemy has to farm under tower and can't leave the lane.
PUSH ➜ · 3/3 · WHY?
You buy time
While they're stuck at their tower, you're free:
