# EP01 — Norway purse seine · sources
| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Typical Norwegian purse seine: ~746 m long × 212 m deep (offshore vessel, 64 m) / 571 × 201 m (coastal) | S1 |
| Illustrative total seine length ~700 m | S2 |
| ~80 large purse seiners in the pelagic fleet | S3 |
| Modern 83 m purse seiner/trawler; RSW tanks 2,755 m³; two 20-inch hydraulic fish pumps | S4 |
| Large pelagic vessels cool catch in refrigerated sea water (RSW) | S5 |
| Autumn mackerel season starts late August NW of Ålesund, moves south to east of Shetland; purse seiners take the largest share | S6, S7 |
| Herring and mackerel behaviour inside a purse seine observed with multibeam sonar | S8 |
| Purse seine: net set around school, bottom closed by hauling purse line through rings | FAO gear sheet |
Avoid: quotas, auctions, slipping/welfare debate, bycatch, disputes.
