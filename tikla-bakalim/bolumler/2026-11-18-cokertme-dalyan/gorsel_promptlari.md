# Çökertme Dalyan — Görsel Promptları (23 görsel)

## Nasıl kullanılır

- Her kareyi **ayrı** üret. Dosya adını karenin numarasıyla kaydet: `01.png`, `02.png` … `24.png` (03 yok).
- Oran **9:16 dikey**, en az **1080×1920**.
- Her promptun başına aşağıdaki **ORTAK STİL** bloğunu, sonuna **NEGATİF** bloğu ekle
  (araç negatif prompt kabul etmiyorsa sonuna "Avoid: ..." diye yaz).
- Karakter geçen karelerde **KARAKTER** tarifini aynen kopyala. Tutarlılık için ilk karakter görselini
  (kare 01) referans görsel olarak sonraki karelere ver.
- Ana konu ekranın **orta %60'ında** olsun: üstteki ~%15 ve alttaki ~%25 alana yazı ve altyazı geliyor.
- Görsele **yazı, logo, filigran koyma** — yazıları ben ekliyorum.
- Kulübe ve düzenek gerçek fotoğraflardan değil haber anlatımından tarif edildi: kulübe ~3 m yüksek,
  kayalıkta ya da deniz üstünde; ağ ~30×20 m, iki direk arasında dipte yatay. Tutarlı kalsın.

### ORTAK STİL (her promptun başına)
```
Photorealistic documentary photograph, vertical 9:16 composition, shot on a full-frame camera,
natural light, Aegean Sea coast of Karaburun peninsula, İzmir, Turkey, late summer (September),
crystal-clear turquoise water, pale limestone rocks, sparse dry maquis shrubs and thyme on the
hillsides, authentic rural Turkish fishing life, subtle film grain, true-to-life colors,
high detail, National Geographic style.
```

### KARAKTER (balıkçı) — geçtiği her karede aynen
```
A Turkish fisherman around 62 years old, deeply sun-tanned weathered face, short grey hair,
grey stubble, thick hands, wearing a faded navy-blue work shirt with rolled-up sleeves,
dark grey trousers, worn plastic sandals, and a faded dark cap.
```

### KARAKTER 2 (yardımcı, eşi) — 06, 07, 21, 22. karelerde
```
His wife, a Turkish fisherwoman in her late 50s, sun-tanned, wearing a wide straw hat over a light
cotton headscarf, long-sleeved faded green shirt, dark trousers, rubber boots.
```

### KEFAL (balık) — balık geçen her karede
```
Flathead grey mullet (Mugil cephalus): torpedo-shaped silver-grey body, darker olive-grey back,
faint dark horizontal stripes along the flanks, broad flat head, small mouth, silvery belly.
```

### NEGATİF (her promptun sonuna)
```
no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no signature, no cartoon, no illustration, no CGI look,
no fishing rods, no modern yachts or speedboats, no tourists, no extra fingers, no deformed hands,
no duplicate people, no oversaturated colors.
```

## Kareler

Sağdaki "Cümle" sütunu, karenin seslendirmede hangi cümleye denk geldiğini gösterir.

### 01 — HOOK · "İzmir Karaburunlu balıkçılar kefali, bütün gün denize bakarak yakalıyor."
```
[ORTAK STİL] [KARAKTER] sitting inside a small weathered wooden lookout hut built on stilts about
3 meters high on top of pale limestone rocks at the water's edge. The hut is made of sun-bleached
planks with a corrugated metal roof and an open front facing the sea. He leans forward on a plank
railing, eyes fixed intently on the turquoise sea below, a small tulip-shaped glass of Turkish tea
next to him. Two thin ropes run from the railing down toward the water. Camera slightly behind and
beside him, over-the-shoulder, so we see his face in profile and the wide calm sea in front of him.
Early morning soft golden side light. [NEGATİF]
```

### 02 — HOOK · "Ama asıl sır, denizin dibinde."
```
[ORTAK STİL] Underwater photograph in clear Aegean water about 4 meters deep. A large rectangular
fishing net made of thin greenish-white mesh lies completely flat on a sandy seabed with scattered
seagrass patches and small rocks. Its edges are held down by a lead line; two thin ropes rise from
the net corners toward the bright surface. Sunbeams and rippling caustic light patterns on the sand.
Above the net, the dark silhouettes of a school of [KEFAL] glide by, slightly blurred. Mysterious,
calm, slightly dark mood, deep blue-green tones. Low angle from the seabed. [NEGATİF]
```

### 04 — Abone · "Böyle merak edilen şeyleri basitçe anlatıyoruz…"
```
[ORTAK STİL] Aerial drone photograph looking down at a rugged rocky stretch of the Karaburun

### 05 — "Bu yöntemin adı çökertme dalyan. Karaburun'da yüz yılı aşkın süredir yaşatılıyor."
```
[ORTAK STİL] Wide eye-level photograph from the water toward the shore: a small rocky cove with the
weathered wooden lookout hut on 3-meter stilts on the limestone rocks, a wooden ladder leading up to
it, a small blue-and-white wooden rowboat pulled up on the pebbles, and two tall wooden poles
standing in the shallow water about 20 meters offshore, ropes sagging from them toward the hut.
Behind: dry hills, a few olive trees, a distant whitewashed village. Morning light, nostalgic,
timeless feeling. [NEGATİF]
```

### 06 — "Sabah erkenden denize iki direk dikiliyor."
```
[ORTAK STİL] [KARAKTER] and [KARAKTER 2] standing in a small blue-and-white wooden rowboat in calm
shallow turquoise water at dawn, together pushing a long weathered wooden pole vertically down into
the seabed. A second identical pole already stands in the water a few meters away. Soft pink and
gold dawn sky, mist over the water, ripples around the pole. Medium shot from water level. [NEGATİF]
```

### 07 — "Kocaman bir ağ, bu direklere bağlanıp denizin dibine seriliyor."
```
[ORTAK STİL] [KARAKTER] and [KARAKTER 2] in the small wooden rowboat between two wooden poles,
feeding a large greenish-white fishing net with a cork float line and a lead line over the side
of the boat; the net sinks into the clear turquoise water, visible spreading below the surface.
One edge of the net is tied to a pole with rope. Early morning light, active working moment,
water droplets on the net. Medium-wide shot from slightly above. [NEGATİF]
```

### 08 — (aynı cümle, su altı) "…denizin dibine seriliyor."
```
[ORTAK STİL] Underwater wide photograph: a large rectangular greenish-white mesh net (about 30 by 20
meters) settling flat onto a sandy seabed between two wooden poles that rise vertically to the
bright water surface. Ropes run from the net's corners up the poles. Sand puffs where the lead line
touches the bottom. Crystal-clear water, sun rays, caustic patterns, a few small fish. Calm,
documentary, slightly elevated diagonal view so the full rectangle of the net is readable. [NEGATİF]
```

### 09 — "Ağ, kefal sürülerinin geçiş yoluna kuruluyor."
```
[ORTAK STİL] Underwater photograph near a rocky shoreline: a large school of [KEFAL] (dozens of fish)
swimming in the same direction along the coast, parallel to the rocks, heading toward the viewer's
right where the edge of a flat net on the seabed is just visible. Shafts of sunlight, clear
blue-green water, rocks with algae on the left. Dynamic but calm. [NEGATİF]
```

### 10 — "İplerin ucu ise kayalıklardaki küçük bir kulübeye uzanıyor."
```
[ORTAK STİL] Close photograph inside the wooden lookout hut: two thick weathered ropes come up from
the sea over the plank railing and are tied around wooden cleats; [KARAKTER]'s hands rest on them.
Through the open front, the turquoise sea and the two wooden poles offshore are visible, the ropes
stretching in a long gentle curve from the hut down to the water and out to the poles. Morning light,
shallow depth of field, focus on the ropes and hands. [NEGATİF]
```

### 11 — (aynı cümle, sistemin tamamı — KESİT)
```
[ORTAK STİL] Realistic split-level half-underwater photograph (over-under shot), the water line
crossing the middle of the frame. Top half: the wooden lookout hut on stilts on the limestone rocks
at the left, two wooden poles in the water, ropes running from the hut to the poles. Bottom half:
clear underwater view of the sandy seabed with the large greenish-white net lying flat between the
bases of the two poles, ropes descending to its corners. Sunlight, crystal-clear water, everything
readable at once. [NEGATİF]
```

### 12 — "Balıkçı kulübeye çıkıyor ve bütün gün bekliyor."
```
[ORTAK STİL] [KARAKTER] climbing a short wooden ladder up into the lookout hut on stilts over the
rocks, carrying a small thermos and a folded cushion; the sea glitters behind him. Late morning

```
[ORTAK STİL] Wide photograph of the lookout hut on stilts on the rocks under a harsh high midday sun,

### 14 — Beğen · "Asıl ilginç kısım şimdi geliyor. İşine yaradıysa bir beğen bırak."

### 15 — "Balıkçı gözünü denizden hiç ayırmıyor."
```
[ORTAK STİL] Point-of-view photograph from inside the lookout hut looking down over the plank railing

### 16 — "Çünkü ağın üstünde bir parıltı görürse, sürü tam oradadır."

[ORTAK STİL] Same point-of-view from the hut as frame 15, but now just under the water surface above

### 18 — "İşte o an, iki ipi aynı anda çekiyor."

[ORTAK STİL] Dynamic close-up inside the hut: [KARAKTER]'s two strong weathered hands gripping two
